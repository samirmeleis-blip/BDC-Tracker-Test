# "The Last Cookie" — A One-Minute Family Skit

**Runtime:** 1:00 exactly · one continuous take
**Cast:** Sammy (the oracle), Alex (the genius volcano), Lena (the perpetrator), Mom (the closer)
**Location:** The connected kitchen / family room / playroom — filmable in one unbroken phone walk.

An animated video version of this skit (`the-last-cookie.mp4`) was rendered from
`skit-video/animation.html`, which recreates the house as a virtual set.

---

## Beat 1 — 0:00–0:06 — Kitchen island. The crime scene.

*Tight on the island counter: an empty plate, crumbs, the birthday balloon at frame edge.
ALEX enters, stops dead, lifts the plate like forensic evidence.*

**ALEX:** I left half a cookie here. HALF. That was a controlled experiment.

## Beat 2 — 0:06–0:16 — Pan to the family room. Sammy on the sectional.

*SAMMY, laptop open, does not look up. Does not blink.*

**SAMMY:** Cookie went down at 3:15. You noticing at 3:42… later than I predicted.
**ALEX:** You KNEW?!
**SAMMY:** I know everything in this house.

## Beat 3 — 0:16–0:26 — The ottoman incident.

*ALEX marches toward the armchairs and clips the yellow ottoman at full stride.
Full stumble. Recovers with maximum dignity.*

**ALEX:** *(terrifyingly calm)* I'm not mad. I am DATA-DRIVEN. *(beat)* …The ottoman moved.
**SAMMY:** *(off-screen)* It didn't.

## Beat 4 — 0:26–0:40 — The armchair. The suspect.

*LENA is draped over the armchair, "asleep," crumbs on her shirt. One eye opens.*

**LENA:** *(maximum sweetness)* Hi Alex. You're my favorite brother.
**ALEX:** *(melting against his will)* …There are crumbs ON your SHIRT.
**LENA:** *(instant switch to cranky)* It was on OUR island. So it's OUR cookie. That's the RULE.
**SAMMY:** *(from the couch)* Orange socks. Chocolate thumbprint. Case closed.

## Beat 5 — 0:40–0:54 — The white cabinets. Mom.

*A cabinet door swings open, revealing MOM — holding a fresh cookie. She was there
the whole time. During her speech, Lena silently slips away toward the playroom.*

**MOM:** Lena — we don't eat our brothers' experiments. Alex — breathe. Sammy —
watching a crime and saying nothing makes you an accomplice.
*(hands Alex the cookie)* I restocked an hour ago. I knew before she did.

## Beat 6 — 0:54–1:00 — The button.

*LENA pops her head out between the playroom folding doors.*

**LENA:** *(sweet again)* Can I have half?
*Alex inhales — the full volcano —*
**SAMMY:** *(deadpan)* Three… two…
**ALEX:** MOOOOM!

*SMASH CUT TO BLACK on one loud piano note from the playroom. 1:00 exactly.*

---

### Performance keys

- Sammy never looks up until "Three… two…"
- Alex's volume goes up as his dignity goes down.
- Lena's sweet-to-cranky flip should be instant, like a light switch.
- Mom doesn't walk in — she's revealed, already there.
